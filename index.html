Responsible communication

Email should be useful, expected, and respectful.

JR Nexa recognizes that business communication tools should be used carefully. The practices below describe the standard we aim to support; individual product controls and availability must be verified before use.

  • Use permission-based contact lists and communicate with people who expect relevant messages.
  • Provide a clear way to unsubscribe and respect unsubscribe requests promptly.
  • Handle bounced addresses so repeated delivery attempts do not continue unnecessarily.
  • Review complaints and remove or suppress contacts when communication is not wanted.
  • Protect contact information and limit access to people and systems that need it.
  • Do not use automation for deceptive, abusive, unlawful, or unsolicited communication.
